# Sales Analysis — Key Findings

## Dataset

This analysis uses a synthetic sample dataset containing 20 orders from January to May 2026. The data is for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes only.

## Key Performance Indicators

* **Total Revenue:** ₹719,700
* **Total Orders:** 20
* **Total Units Sold:** 121
* **Average Revenue per Order:** ₹35,985

## Product Performance

1. Laptop — ₹495,000
2. Monitor — ₹132,000
3. Headphones — ₹34,200
4. Keyboard — ₹30,000
5. Mouse — ₹28,500

## Monthly Performance

* January: ₹157,000
* February: ₹100,900
* March: ₹177,200
* April: ₹80,800
* May: ₹203,800

## Business Insights

* Laptops contributed approximately 68.8% of total revenue.
* May recorded the highest monthly revenue.
* April recorded the lowest monthly revenue.
* Revenue varied across the five months, suggesting that monthly performance should be monitored.

## Recommendations

* Monitor laptop sales and product availability because laptops contribute the largest share of revenue.
* Investigate the reasons for the April revenue decline.
* Review monthly product and regional performance before making inventory or marketing decisions.

## Limitations

These findings are based on a small synthetic dataset and do not represent actual company performance. More data would be needed to draw reliable business conclusions.
